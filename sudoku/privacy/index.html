Privacy Policy – Sudoku

FreeMakerBuilds · App ID: com.freemakerbuilds.sudoku · Last updated: November 21, 2026

Sudoku does not collect, share or sell any personal data. This page explains what the app stores and where.

What the app does not do

  • It has no internet access. It does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • It has no accounts, no sign-in and no ads.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • It does not request any special permissions (no location, contacts, camera, microphone or files).

What is stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your phone only:

  • Your settings, such as theme, background and input mode.
  • Puzzles in progress, so you can continue where you left off.
  • Your game history and best times.

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete your history and best times with “Reset scores” in Settings, and all app data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android’s own backup on your phone, Android may include the app’s settings and game data in your personal device backup (for example to your Google account), so they can be restored on a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google under your own account and settings, not by FreeMakerBuilds. We never have access to it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
